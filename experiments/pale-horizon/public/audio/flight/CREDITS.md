# Pale Horizon flight audio

All source recordings below were published under the
[CC0 1.0 Universal dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
on Freesound. CC0 permits copying, modification, distribution, and commercial
use without required attribution. Credits remain here so the recordings can be
traced back to their creators.

Source pages and licence labels were checked on 10 August 2026. Freesound keeps
original downloads behind an account, so the runtime edits use the public HQ
previews served by Freesound's own CDN.

The runtime files are edited derivatives of Freesound's public high-quality MP3
previews. They were trimmed, filtered, loudness-normalized, crossfaded where
looping, resampled to 48 kHz, and encoded as MP3 for broad browser playback.
