Not quite a game.
Not just a scene.

Sansara is an evolving playground of quiet 3D spaces. Each room has its own weather, scale, and strange little logic—but the same character wanders through them all.

There is no score to chase. Travel, look around, test the edges. The project changes as new ideas, places, and ways of moving find their way in.
