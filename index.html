What's interesting in here

  • Dunes aren't noise. Real dunes are asymmetric — a long windward climb and a short, steep slipface. The terrain uses that profile directly instead of symmetric fbm, which is the difference between "desert" and "lumpy beach".
  • Root motion has to go. Mixamo clips downloaded without "In Place" travel ~85 units forward per walk cycle and fight the controller. A small script flattens that out of the exported GLB.
  • Skinned meshes lie about their bounds. Box3.setFromObject measures the bind pose, so grounding a character by its bounding box floats it half a body height off the floor. The foot bones are the source of truth.
  • Draw calls, not polygons. Hundreds of props render in ~50 draw calls via InstancedMesh, with one shadow-casting light and no post-processing — enough to hold 60 fps on a fanless laptop.

Running it

The pages load models over fetch, so they need a server rather than file://:
